### Changed

- Scrapers: the polite fetcher retries a timeout, network error, 429 or 5xx up to 2 times (robots.txt included), waiting `Retry-After` or 10 s then 20 s, within a run-wide budget of 8 retries (at most 12 min added to a run); a `Retry-After` over 60 s is not waited out (was honored once up to 120 s); 4xx other than 429, robots blocks, the body cap and redirect limit are not retried. A host now stops after 3 consecutive URLs that fail after their retries, timeouts and network errors included (was 3 consecutive 5xx), and a 429 or 503 stops it only once its retries are spent (was after one retry). A robots.txt that still answers 429 now disallows the host for the run, like a 5xx (was allow-all). `FetchStats.requests` counts every attempt. A listing that still fails keeps failing its source (exit 2, unchanged). Prompted by a single keengamer child-sitemap timeout failing the first server run.

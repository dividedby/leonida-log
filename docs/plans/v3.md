## 3. Schema and data (test-first)

- `lib/curated.ts`: `kind` (`county`, `region` or `district`) on areas. A county has no parent; a region's parent is a county (required); a district's parent is a region or a county; a child is never visible before its parent. Replaces the one-level "a parent has no parent" rule. Data only, so no DB migration.
- `data/curated/areas.yaml`, each with `slug`, `name`, `visible_after_mission`, `sources[]` and optional `evidence` and `aliases`:
  - Five counties, all `prologue`, named in full: Kelly County, Vice-Dale County, Mariana County, Lummox County and Leonard County. No Ambrosia County, and no bare-word aliases ("Kelly"). Each carries `best-guess` evidence (ADR 0008) on its own basis: Kelly, Mariana and Vice-Dale on gta.wiki plus the I-404 road signs; Lummox on gta.wiki plus the leaked map; Leonard on gta.wiki only.
  - The six regions get county parents: Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia in Kelly, Vice City in Vice-Dale, Leonida Keys and Grassrivers in Mariana, Mount Kalaga National Park in Lummox.
  - Vice City's districts from gta.wiki (https://gta.wiki/w/Vice_City), Vice Beach included. Districts don't nest: Washington Beach is not a district, entries there are placed on Vice Beach, and "Washington Beach" stays in their location text. The Vice Beach row's evidence note records the source conflict (gta.wiki's Vice Beach page: a separate city in Vice-Dale; its Vice City page: not listed; its Kelly County page: a Vice City neighbourhood) and the owner's ruling. The final list and any generic-name qualification are settled here, with sources.
  - The towns current entries need, directly under their county: Capri (Mariana), Millport (Lummox) and Hamlet (Vice-Dale).
  - Leonard has no region and no district yet; areas are added as entries need them.
- Text rule (`lib/curated.data.test.ts`): extends to county and district names and aliases. A generic district name that collides with ordinary words ("Downtown") gets a qualified name or an alias-free entry; each case is decided here.
- Tests: `kind` and parent rules with failing-then-passing cases on the fixture; the text rule on county and district names.
- Done when: the rules have failing-then-passing tests, the real data parses, the text rule passes on all four gates' items and optionals, and no existing placement is broken (an existing `area` still resolves). docs/design.md's two `areas.yaml` bullets (v2 and v3) become one bullet describing the schema as built.

## 4. View models (test-first)

- `lib/map.ts`: `mapView` and `areaSheet` move from "no parent means region" to `kind`. `mapView` gives the counties (label, link), the regions (reached flag, badge rolling up districts) and the Anywhere rows (entries with no place at all). `areaSheet` serves a county (county-level rows, its own plus its county-parented districts' rolled up, and links to its regions with their badges; no county badge), a region (its rows with its districts' rows rolled up, the list of its districts with badges linking to their sheets, the district-map data where one exists) and a district (its own sheet). Null for an unknown, hidden or not-yet-visible slug, including a district whose parent is not visible.
- `areaTag` gives the finest name only; `itemDetail` carries the full path (finest to county, each level a slug and name) only while visible.
- Leak tests: every mission as the current one on the real data, extended to counties and districts, and on a fixture with a district hidden until a later mission (all-prologue real data proves nothing on its own). Fail on a later area name or slug at any level, a later mission title, an unopened optional, a hidden entry or any locked count.
- Done when: fixture tests cover reached and unreached districts, county-level rows, rollup, the path, the finest-name tag, Anywhere and the 404 cases; leak tests pass on both data sets.

## Risks to watch

- **County and district names as spoilers:** a district reached late named early, in the map, a tag, a path, item text or a URL. Mitigated by per-area visibility, the text rule at every level and leak tests on a fixture with a later district.
- **Generic district names in the text rule:** "Downtown", "Southside" and similar collide with ordinary words, so the whole-word rule can false-positive or force awkward text. Decided per case in step 3; an unfixable name gets a qualified name or no alias.
- **A drawing that ends up traced:** more detail (a closer coastline, district shapes) pulls toward copying a game, fan or real-world map. Draw from the sources' descriptions, keep it schematic and not to scale, and keep no reference image in the repo.
- **Phone width with more labels:** county labels, region names, badges and district names at 375px, at larger text sizes. The mockup checks it before any code (step 2).
- **A deep hierarchy:** three levels make tags, paths and rollups harder to read and to test. Keep to the rule that a district's parent is a region or a county and districts don't nest (Washington Beach is placed on Vice Beach).
- **Thin county evidence:** membership rests on one fan wiki, road signs for three counties and the leaked map for one, recorded as `best-guess` and accepted for shipping; re-checked weekly (ADR 0008).

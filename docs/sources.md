# Sources: ranking and scraper plan

Which guide sites and data sources to trust, how they copy each other, and which ones get a scraper (ADR 0007). Updated 2026-11-26 (CT). The data behind it sits in the gitignored `research_notes/` folder and isn't committed.

## How the ranking was computed

- **Evidence counts.** For each domain, the number of findings it backs that survived the skeptics: round 6 items with final grade `READY` (266) and round 7 items graded `READY` (124). Counts come from `research_notes/GTA 6 tracker data round 6/_data/round6-results.json` and `research_notes/GTA 6 tracker data round 7/round7-results.json`, one count per item per origin.
- **Refuted counts.** Items the domain was cited on that ended `REFUTED` in round 6 (70), plus items where any skeptic vote came back `refuted: true` without a final refutation (both rounds). These count co-citation, not blame: a refuted item often cites a good source next to a bad one.
- **Copy chains collapsed.** Copies are counted under their origin, so an item citing both PowerPyx and gtabase counts once. The chains come from `2-source-independence-audit.md` (round 6) and the round 6 and 7 skeptic notes:
  - gtabase → PowerPyx. gtabase pages are timestamped 5 to 105 minutes after PowerPyx's and paraphrase them. Bad Driver! is a rare exception where gtabase came first.
  - The Click, Game Checklists and gta6bugs → the PowerPyx/gtabase chain. They're aggregators.
  - gta6heists → YouTube footage. Its wording is its own, but it's second-hand and names no creator.
  - gta6pedia → Polygon. Leonida Guide → Rare Thief. Leonida Streets and Gamertag Mythras → KeenGamer.
  - Game8 JP and Game8 EN have the same owner, but their missables lists differ, so they're kept apart. Game8 JP counts on its own only for claims Game8 EN doesn't make.
- **Chain counts.** An origin's row counts every item backed by the origin or by any of its copies. An item that cites only gtabase still counts toward the PowerPyx chain. So the chain total (193 / 102) is higher than PowerPyx's own citations (148 / 81).
- **Columns.** "READY r6 / r7 (sole)": `READY` items in round 6 / round 7, and in brackets the `READY` items where this origin was the only one cited. "Refuted / skeptic-refuted": items with final `REFUTED` in round 6 / items in either round where a skeptic voted `refuted: true` but the item was not finally refuted.
- **Wayback.** Archived copies (`web.archive.org/web/...`) count toward the archived site. `web.archive.org` itself is a fetch method, not a source.
- **Fetch method and robots.txt** were checked live on 2026-11-26 with a generic User-Agent (`LeonidaLogBot/0.1`), or come from round 6 `3-scraping-terms-of-sources.md` where marked (r6).
- **Discovery pass.** On 2026-11-26 a 14-agent pass (7 language groups, each followed by a skeptic) checked 33 sites we hadn't used. Results are in [New candidates](#new-candidates). They have no track record yet, so they rank by tier only.

Tiers:

- **A**: primary. Staff- or first-hand-played, with per-mission pages. One explicit A source can carry a claim.
- **B**: solid corroboration. Thinner, partly derivative, or narrow in scope.
- **C**: leads only. Never the one source for a claim.
- **X**: never counts: AI-drafted, or a copy with no origin of its own.

## Never trust alone (tier X)

- **AI-drafted:** The Games Wiki, egamers, gaming.net, pro-gamer.gg, happygamer, gtasixvicecity, gta6story.org, gtadb.io. Rare Thief and Leonida Guide are possibly AI-assisted (round 6 skeptic).
- **Copies:** gtabase (of PowerPyx; see row 1a), The Click, Game Checklists, gta6bugs, gta6pedia (of Polygon), Leonida Guide (of Rare Thief), Leonida Streets and Gamertag Mythras (of KeenGamer). gta6heists is footage-derived and counts as corroboration only.
- Game Checklists' "players report" lines are still useful as contradiction signals when they _diverge_ from the PowerPyx chain.

## Scraper plan

The first scrapers, in rank order: PowerPyx, gtabase, IGN, KeenGamer, Game8 JP, GameWith and Vandal, then the GitHub datamines (REST API only; the `github` source watches files by commit sha and reports a weekly repo search for review), plus a Wayback CDX helper for hosts that block plain HTTP. Each one is a module in `scrapers/sources/` that can be switched off on its own (ADR 0007). The next batch, after a round that cites them: gry-online, 3dmgame, 3DJuegos, gameblog, kamigame and 4gamers.

Every scraper keeps to these rules:

- It sends a generic, descriptive User-Agent that never contains `Claude`, `Code` or `Cursor`, and never imitates a browser.
- It respects the machine rules in robots.txt.
- It stores extracted facts and links on the server only (ADR 0003).
- If a site objects, delete its module and purge its candidates.
- IGN, KeenGamer and Polygon ban automated access in their terms, and IGN also does so in a robots.txt comment. ADR 0007 records the owner's choice of breadth over a terms allowlist. IGN's and KeenGamer's modules are the first to go if either one objects.
